I am a Brazilian living in Germany. For anyone coming from an underdeveloped country, that is almost a dream come true. Every time you think about that, please remember your beloved parents, who are 10 000 km away from you and whom you will not see on your birthday, Easter, or Christmas. Yes, you have spent the last 30 occasions with them, but now you will be lucky to see them in a few of them in the next many years.

As I said, dream come true..........
