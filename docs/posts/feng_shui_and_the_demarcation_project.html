Review of the Paper "Feng Shui and the Demarcation Project"

Some time ago, I sent the paper Feng Shui and the Demarcation Project, by Damian Fernandez-Beanato, to my friend Ruan Comelli. I told him that I can separate science from pseudo-science. However, even after a Bachelor and a Master's degree, I still struggle to argue and convince people what is and what is not science. I do think I am not alone!

Ruan asked me tell him what I think about the paper, and he suggested me to write a blog post intead of writing to him.
