Difficult Phonemes

Introduction

One of the things I find most difficult in learning a new language (I am learning German) is to learn new phonemes. The other day I was learning the difference between o, u, ö, and ü. In short, it feels IMPOSSIBLE to differenciate them (mainly ö and ü). But I know the reason: my native language, Portuguese, doesn't have the respective phonemes: y, ʏ, ø (it seems that English has, but I guess English doesn't rely on that differentiation to differentiate words, that's why I have never noticed...).

Because of that, I decided to make a list of pairs of words that are hard for native speakers of English and/or portuguese to differentiate.

Brazilian Portuguese Words

I had a german friend who learned Portuguese and an English relative who tries to repeate Portuguese words. I notice that both have difficulties with some Portuguese words, and I know why: they do not have the phonemes in their language. So, aditionally, I made a list of words that are difficult for non-native speakers of Portuguese to pronounce correctly (or even differentiate), simply to show that everyone suffers with that when learning a foreign language (in this case, Portuguese).
